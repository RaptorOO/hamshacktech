How ICR works

When a character plays, press the space bar the instant you recognize it, then type the character you heard. (Practicing numbers on a phone? Just tap the number on the pad — no space bar needed.)

Car Mode

Listen only — nothing to type. Each character plays five times, with time after each play to say it out loud (Max Time To Answer, below), then the next one. Keeps playing if the screen turns off. Uses this page’s tone and volume.

Listen-only practice for a walk or hike. Each character plays five times, with time after each play to say it (Max Time To Answer, below); after the fifth, a voice says the character so you know if you got it. It keeps playing with the screen off and the phone in your pocket. Earbuds recommended.
