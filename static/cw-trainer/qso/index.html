How this works

The other station sends at your Settings speed (the gear icon), and you answer with your key. End each of your overs the usual way — K, KN, BK or SK — then pause, or press Enter. If you stop without one, a few seconds’ pause ends your over.

It’s forgiving like a real operator: order doesn’t matter, repeats are fine, and a call sign with one letter off still gets through. If something’s missing they’ll ask — QRZ? (who’s calling?), NAME?, RST?, PSE AGN — or QRS if they can’t read you. You can ask them too: send AGN or ? to hear their last over again, or QRS to have them slow down.

Standard QSO: call signs, signal report, name and QTH, then 73. POTA: the quick Parks on the Air exchange — the activator calls CQ POTA with the park number, hunters send just their call, each side sends 5NN (599) and state, then TU.
