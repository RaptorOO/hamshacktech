Practice the placement test

At the end of Fundamental, CW Academy checks your copy before placing you in Intermediate. Each question sends a call sign or a short word or phrase in Morse; then you pick what you heard from 4 choices of the same length. The test sends 25 WPM characters with Farnsworth spacing for an overall speed of about 10 WPM. You need 80% to pass.
