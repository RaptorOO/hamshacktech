    How this works

    Copy this: send what’s shown, then pause. When you stop for a word space (7 dits) after the last word, it’s graded. Enter grades it now; Esc starts it over.

    The timing rules: a dah is 3 dits long; the gap inside a letter is 1 dit; between letters 3 dits; between words 7 dits. “Character speed” is how fast your dits and dahs go. “Overall speed” includes your spacing — what someone copying you actually hears. Extra space between letters (Farnsworth spacing) is fine while learning, but the closer you get to 3 and 7, the easier you are to copy.

    With paddles the keyer makes perfect dits and dahs, so the useful feedback is spacing and which letters came out wrong. With a straight key or bug you form the elements yourself, so you’ll also see your dah-to-dit ratio (ideally 3:1).
