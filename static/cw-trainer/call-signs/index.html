Call Signs

About This Trainer

Four ways to practice copying call signs, the thing you copy most on the air. Every call is made up fresh, following the real formats (K6AB, KA6ABC, G4ABC, VE3XYZ…), so there's no list to memorize.

Find the Number: a call plays every few seconds until you type its number. The fewer plays you need, the more points (5 for the first play, down to 1). Call Sign Builder: short three-character calls, a letter first, then a number and a letter in either order. Call Sign Simulator: every call comes at a different speed, somewhere between the slowest and fastest you choose, like the stations you'll hear on the bands. Speed Run: get a call right and the next one is 1 WPM faster; miss it and it's 2 WPM slower. Faster and longer calls score more.

After a miss the trainer waits with the answer showing: press Replay to hear it again at the same speed, then Next, Enter or Space. Head Copy Mode blocks typing until the call has finished. The Timer chip sets how long to practice.
