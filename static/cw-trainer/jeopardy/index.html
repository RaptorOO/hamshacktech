      How to play

      Everyone joins from their own CW Trainer, hears each clue on their own device at their own speed, and answers with their key. To play by yourself, host a game and start it with just you.

      Background

      • The game was designed not to be competitive as players are at different stages of their morse code journey. It is about having fun.
      • The game displays a Group Score and Tiles You Copied. Tiles You Copied is only visible to you.

      Turns and scoring

      • Turns go in the order people joined, host first. When it's yours, you'll see "It's your turn" and hear your call sign in Morse. Pick any tile. After 30 seconds you get a reminder.
      • The tile zooms out to fill the screen. After "Stand by · 3, 2, 1" you hear VVV, then the answer in Morse. Everyone hears it on their own device at their own speed. Each dollar row is a little faster than the one above it.
      • When your clue ends, send the answer with your key: a Vail or VBand adapter, the [ and ] keys, or the DIT and DAH pads on a phone. Your copy appears on the card as you send, with the spacing you sent.
      • When you're done, tap Done, press Enter or send AR. Replay plays the clue again (only you hear it). Clear wipes your answer so you can send it again. The first two times on a clue, Clear gives everyone 15 more seconds.
      • Everyone gets 60 seconds, counted from when the slowest player's clue ends. The answer is shown when time runs out or when everyone has answered.
      • The letters must be exact, but spacing between words isn't marked wrong.
      • The group earns the tile's value times the share of players who copied it. For example, 2 of 4 correct earns half.
      • Tiles You Copied, next to the Group Score, adds the full value of each tile you get right. It isn’t part of the Group Score, so it can be bigger. Only you can see it.
      • Each column hides one Daily Double, worth double points.
      • Use My speed to change your listening and sending speeds during the game.
      • The host can Pause and Resume the game, skip a turn or end the game. While paused, the clock stops. A clue that was still playing starts over from Stand by.

      Sound

      Your listening and sending speeds are set when you host or join, and with My speed during the game.
