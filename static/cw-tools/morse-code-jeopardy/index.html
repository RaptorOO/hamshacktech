Host Instructions

To share the game on Zoom

  • Open Morse Code Jeopardy in a browser window
  • On Zoom, click the Share Screen button at the bottom
  • Select just the Application Window that shows your browser screen with Jeopardy loaded
  • On the right panel, select Share Sound. IF YOU DO NOT DO THIS, PLAYERS WILL NOT BE ABLE TO HEAR CODE!

How to play

  • THIS GAME IS NOT COMPETITIVE AND INDIVIDUAL PLAYERS ARE NOT SCORED. THIS IS TO HELP OUR GROUP HAVE FUN.
  • Players take turns calling a category and dollar amount.
  • After a 3-second delay, the hidden answer plays in Morse — up to three times (original speed, then two slower plays).
  • If the group copies it on the first play, click Got it for 100% of the tile. Second play = 50%. Third play = 25%.
  • Each column hides one Daily Double. That player must think of an answer that would be fitting for that category and send it to the group in CW. The group copies live and the tile is scored like other tiles depending on how many times the player has to send the code.
  • After Reveal, click the answer to hear it again.
