CW Code Groups Trainer

About This Trainer

Listen to a group of characters, then type what you heard before the clock runs out. Random groups break the habit of counting dits and dahs by copy-writing — you're forced to hold the whole group in your head and write it after the fact, the same way you'll copy a callsign or a QSO on the air.

After a missed group, the trainer waits with the answer showing: press Replay to hear what you missed as many times as you like, then Next, Enter, or Space to continue. Head Copy Mode blocks typing until the whole group has finished playing. The Call Signs group invents brand-new US and international calls every round, so there's no list to memorize.

Common Words mixes the most frequent English words with on-the-air ham vocabulary (QRP, FB, YAESU, EFHW…). Word Pool sets how deep into the frequency list to go — start with the Top 100 and widen it as your copy improves. Words and QSO Elements are dealt like a shuffled deck: nothing repeats until you've heard every one at that setting.

The Elapsed stopwatch counts up from Start Session. Tap Timer to choose how long to practice; when it runs out, a bell rings and the session stops.
