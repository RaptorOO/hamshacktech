**Level up your ham shack with tech.** HamShackTech is a home for practical write-ups (and, soon, video) on bringing modern technology into the ham shack — operating your rig through a networked or remote browser session, modding radios to unlock extra functionality, automating shack gear and software with a Stream Deck, and putting AI tools to work improving how you operate.

Start with [Guides](/guides/) for step-by-step tutorials, browse [Reviews](/reviews/) of gear and software, or check the [Blog](/blog/) for project logs and everything else.
