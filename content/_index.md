**Level up your ham shack with tech.** HamShackTech is your home for practical write-ups (and, soon, video) on bringing modern technology into your ham shack — operating your rig remotely, modding HT radios to unlock extra functionality, automating shack gear and software with Stream Deck, Node-Red, Home Assistant and other platforms, and leveraging modern AI tools to operate more effectively and faster.

Start with [Guides](/guides/) for step-by-step tutorials, browse [Reviews](/reviews/) of gear and software, or check the [Blog](/blog/) for project logs and everything else.
