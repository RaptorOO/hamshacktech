Practical, no-install tools for CW practice and operating — built to run right in your browser, on desktop or phone. More will land here over time; first up is a trainer for Instant Character Recognition.

<div class="tool-card">
  <div class="tool-card-header">
    <h3>CW ICR Trainer</h3>
    <span class="tool-card-badge">Free · Browser App</span>
  </div>

  <p>Drills Instant Character Recognition (ICR) — the skill of recognizing a Morse character the moment you hear it, rather than counting dits and dahs. Set your own speed (WPM), tone, and volume, and drill letters, numbers, punctuation, or a custom character set. A configurable Max Time to Answer keeps you honest about "instant," and the app automatically tracks which characters are giving you the most trouble — weighting practice toward them and offering a one-tap drill on your five weakest characters.</p>

  <h4>How to use it</h4>
  <ol>
    <li>Open the trainer and tap <strong>Start Session</strong> — your keyboard opens once and stays up for the whole session.</li>
    <li>Pick a <strong>Practice Group</strong> (Letters, Numbers, Punctuation, All, or a Custom set) and set WPM, Tone, Volume, and Max Time to Answer under Console Settings.</li>
    <li>When a character plays, press the <strong>space bar</strong> the instant you recognize it, then type the character you heard.</li>
    <li>Check <strong>Your Worst Characters</strong> any time and tap <strong>Drill These Characters</strong> for focused repetition on your weak points.</li>
  </ol>
