Everything for CW practice now lives in one free app: the **HamShackTech CW Trainer**. Install it on your computer or phone and it works like any other app, even with no internet connection.

  <div class="tool-card-header">
    <h3>HamShackTech CW Trainer App</h3>
    <span class="tool-card-badge">Free · Installable App</span>
  </div>

  <p>One app you use online, or install on your computer, phone or tablet. Installed, it gets its own icon and its own window, and it works with no internet connection. Updates arrive automatically whenever you open it while you're online. Inside are five tabs: <strong>Copy</strong> (ICR, Code Groups and Call Signs), <strong>Send</strong> (Keyer Practice and QSO Practice), <strong>Course</strong> (My Course and Test Prep), <strong>Play</strong> (Jeopardy) and <strong>Progress</strong>. The tools:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>ICR</strong>: Instant Character Recognition drills. Hear a character, press the space bar the moment you know it, then type it. Tracks your weakest characters and drills them, Car Mode plays characters for hands-free listening, and Walk Mode does the same with a voice that says each one, playing on with the phone in your pocket.</li>
    <li><strong>Code Groups</strong>: head copy of character groups, common words, QSO elements, call signs, phrases and sayings, or your own text file (like a Morserino practice file), with Farnsworth spacing and a replay after each miss. Walk Mode plays them hands-free, with a voice that spells each one out.</li>
    <li><strong>Keyer</strong>: sending practice with your own key through a Vail or VBand adapter (or the on-screen paddles), with your speed, spacing and timing measured, including Farnsworth spacing like 25/10. Copy this can also send the letter pyramid, QSO exchanges, the dits exercise, pangrams or your own text, line by line.</li>
    <li><strong>QSO</strong>: work a complete contact (standard, POTA, SOTA or a CWops CWT) with a virtual station, using your key. Set separate copy and sending speeds.</li>
    <li><strong>Test Prep</strong>: practice the CW Academy placement test for Intermediate, the way it actually runs: 8 questions (5 with two everyday words, 3 US call signs), each sent once at 25 WPM (Farnsworth 10), then 20 seconds to pick what you heard from five suggested answers for each word and call sign. Every word and call sign is marked right or wrong on its own, and you get your score out of 13.</li>
    <li><strong>Call Signs</strong>: four ways to copy call signs. Find the Number (type the call's number in as few plays as you can), Call Sign Builder (short three-character calls), Call Sign Simulator (every call at a different speed, in the range you choose) and Speed Run (each call you copy sends the next one faster).</li>
    <li><strong>My Course</strong>: for CW Academy students, your class schedule (all 16 classes, with dates once you enter your first class) and links to your curriculum and practice files. Choose your course and level in Settings.</li>
    <li><strong>Progress</strong>: your practice time, day streaks, accuracy and speed trends, and the characters you miss most.</li>
    <li><strong>Jeopardy</strong>: Morse Code Jeopardy for CW groups. Play online with up to 20 people, or on your own, each hearing the clues at their own speed and answering with their key. Everyone builds one group score together.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Each tool keeps its own speed, tone and volume, so your ICR speed doesn&rsquo;t change your QSO speed. Sign in with your email (optional, no password) and your practice history follows you to all your devices.</p>

  <h4>How to install it</h4>
  <ol>
    <li>Open the app in <strong>Microsoft Edge</strong> or <strong>Google Chrome</strong> (Windows, Mac, ChromeOS, or Android) and click <strong>Install app</strong> in its title bar — or the install icon at the right end of the browser's address bar.</li>
    <li>On an iPhone or iPad, open it in Safari, tap <strong>Share</strong>, then <strong>Add to Home Screen</strong>.</li>
    <li>On a Mac with Safari (macOS Sonoma or later), open it and choose <strong>File → Add to Dock</strong>.</li>
    <li>Launch it from your Start menu, dock, or home screen like any other app.</li>
  </ol>
