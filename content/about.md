I'm a licensed amateur radio operator with a growing interest in
pairing traditional ham radio with modern technology — operating
a station remotely over a network, modifying radios for capabilities
the manufacturer didn't ship, automating a shack full of gear and
software with a Stream Deck, and experimenting with AI tools to
operate smarter. This site (and an accompanying YouTube channel) will
document projects, lessons learned, and practical guides —
written for operators who are comfortable with a soldering iron but
new to things like scripting and automation (and vice versa).
