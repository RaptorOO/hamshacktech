About This Site

I'm a licensed amateur radio operator with a growing interest in pairing traditional ham radio with modern home automation tools like Home Assistant. This site will document projects, lessons learned, and practical guides — written for operators who are comfortable with a soldering iron but new to things like YAML configs, automations, and network integrations (and vice versa).
